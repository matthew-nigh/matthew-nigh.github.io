# Figures and sources

Only the figures and qualitative outcomes below are used. No numerical performance gains are claimed.

- **Synthetic PCM research card:** validation using E-test data from approximately **8,000 wafers** in an advanced GlobalFoundries FinFET node; **Best Paper Honorable Mention** at ITC 2024. The award is also the TRELA Experience outcome. Source: user-verified ITC 2024 facts, “Generation and Quality Evaluation of Synthetic Process Control Monitoring Data,” [doi:10.1109/ITC51657.2024.00042](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITC51657.2024.00042).
- **Synthetic PCM research card:** correlation-aware synthetic data generation for WAT limit setting (ITC 2026); qualitative only. Full author list in the publication override: Matthew Nigh, Aric Fowler, Gabrielle Geppert, Ching-Yi Chang, John Carulli, Yiorgos Makris. Source: user-verified [ITC 2026 program](https://easychair.org/smart-program/ITC2026/). No DOI is asserted; the program link wins even if a future catalog refresh supplies a DOI URL.
- **Metrology-to-E-Test research card:** correlation modeling integrating process expertise; qualitative only, retained from existing content. Source: [doi:10.1109/VTS65138.2025.11022777](https://doi.org/10.1109/VTS65138.2025.11022777).
- **WALET research card:** SHAP-guided classification of wafer-level e-test variability; qualitative only, retained from existing content. Source: [doi:10.1109/VTS69484.2026.11563333](https://doi.org/10.1109/VTS69484.2026.11563333).

Source: user-verified DAC 2026 abstract, [doi:10.1145/3770743.3804428](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770743.3804428). Full author names are preserved in the display override.

Source: the user-verified experience facts in the task brief and existing repository content. Organization URL (context, not independent verification of the personal role): [Advantest](https://www.advantest.com/).
